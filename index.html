Peanut Butter Cookies

3 ingredients350°FAbout 12 minutes

“This recipe is super duper simple, and it's so good for just an after-school snack.”

  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg

Mix everything in one bowl, roll the dough into little balls on parchment paper, and bake at 350° for about 12 minutes. Press a fork across the top first if you want them to look cute.

The dough should hold a cookie shape. If it's runny, add a little more sugar: these are cookies, not a cake.

Chocolate Mug Cake

One mugMicrowaveAbout 2 minutes

“Nothing says ambition like a chocolate mug cake.”

  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ cup milk
  • Chocolate chips, if you want them

Stir everything together right in the mug, drop in a few chocolate chips, and microwave for about 2 minutes.

This batter should be liquidy, because it's a cake. Read the spoon twice: tablespoons and teaspoons are not the same thing.

What staying out of the kitchen costs

Without baking

  • You miss the feeling of having made something yourself.
  • You miss out on something totally delicious.
  • You spend a ton on takeout and DoorDash for treats you could make at home.

With a few easy recipes

  • You learn a basic skill that stays with you for life.
  • You feel productive and proud of what you made.
  • You become the friend who brings the cookies.
